Who we are

A client-focused design studio, from first sketch to final styling.

Sabanas & Mueble Limited is a full-service interior design firm based in Lagos. We build every project around the people who'll actually live in it — which is where our bespoke approach and our reputation for satisfied clients both come from.

Design, art and architecture sit at the centre of what we do. The result is spaces that are beautiful without giving up function, and events that feel considered down to the last detail.
