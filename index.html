Who we are

A design and lifestyle company, built around how you live.

Sabanas & Mueble is a Lagos-based design and lifestyle company built around a simple idea: good design should make life better. Our work sits at the intersection of design, art, architecture, comfort and functionality.

We listen, understand, curate and execute with intention — whether we're transforming a home, styling an event, sourcing a stay, arranging mobility or helping a client find a property. Our goal isn't simply to deliver a service, it's to make the entire experience feel seamless.

Where it began

From drab to fab, as we like to say.

Growing up surrounded by artists, creatives and entrepreneurs, our founder was exposed early to the beauty of imagination, craftsmanship and creating something from nothing — the foundation of everything Sabanas & Mueble has become.

"Good design isn't simply about making something look beautiful. It's about understanding people — how they live, what they need, what they value — and creating something that improves the way they experience their space."

Every project is still a small reminder of that transformative power: the moment a client sees their space for the first time, and the idea they'd only imagined becomes tangible.
